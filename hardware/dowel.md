# Dowel

Fluted beech wood dowels in two lengths: Ø8 × 40 for the carcass, Ø8 × 30 for the drawers.

## Interface

Everything downstream depends only on these. Changing the product means updating these
values and checking the constraints.

- Wd: dowel diameter = 8
- Wh: hole depth into an edge, or into a T1 face = 21
- Wf: hole depth into a T2 face = 11

Constraints, centred in a T2 edge:

- Wd ≤ T2 - 6
- Wh ≤ T1 - 8
- Wf ≤ T2 - 4

## Shape

- Carcass: Ø8 × 40 cylinder, 20 in each board.
- Drawer: Ø8 × 30 cylinder, 20 in the edge board and 10 in the face board.

## Product

- Carcass: Ø8 × 40, glued in the T2 board only, so the joint can still be knocked down.
  Needed: 12, plus spares.
- Drawers: Ø8 × 30, glued on both sides. Needed: 32, plus spares.
